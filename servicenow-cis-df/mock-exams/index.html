📋 Exam Tips
Flag & Review
Use the 🚩 flag button to mark uncertain questions. Come back via the dot navigator before submitting.
Multi-Select
Questions saying "Select TWO" require exactly that many answers. Partial credit is not awarded — all correct options must be chosen.
Timer Warning
Timer turns amber at 15 minutes remaining, red at 5 minutes. At 00:00 the exam auto-submits with current answers.
Domain Focus
GOVERN is 35% of the exam. If short on time, prioritize GOVERN questions — they carry the most weight.
